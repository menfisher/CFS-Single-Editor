Google saving requires a connected Google account on the Google Connect page. Local saving always remains on.

Shared folder

Choose a folder to grant ContactsFreeShare access, or paste the long folder ID from Google Drive. Use the Shared with me tab for folders another user shared with you.

If Google shows 403 or cannot access your account: Reconnect Google on the Google Connect page after updating ContactsFreeShare. In Google Cloud Console, enable the Google Picker API and restrict GOOGLE_PICKER_API_KEY to HTTP referrer http://127.0.0.1:8000/* (plus http://localhost:8000/* if you use localhost). GOOGLE_PICKER_APP_ID must be your Google Cloud project number, not the OAuth client ID. Close the Google window, allow third-party cookies for ContactsFreeShare, then choose the folder again. Chrome or Edge: click the cookie/eye icon in the address bar and allow third-party cookies for this site, or go to Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies > Sites allowed to use third-party cookies and add 127.0.0.1. Safari: go to Safari > Settings > Privacy, turn off Prevent cross-site tracking, and make sure Block all cookies is off. Private or Incognito windows may still block the Google folder picker.

The folder ID is required for folders outside the app-created ContactsFreeShare folder. Name-only lookup is not used with limited Drive file access.
